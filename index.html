Metropolitan Branch Trail
2 – 5 mi Paved From your door
›
Route Steps
1
Start at Brookland-CUA Metro
MBT trailhead is right at the station. Head south on the paved trail.
2
Run to your goal distance ÷ 2
2 mi total? Turn at mile 1. 4 mi total? Turn at mile 2. Pass NoMa station at ~mile 1.7.

3

Same trail back. Flat, well-lit, vibrant murals along the way.
✅Start and finish at Brookland Metro — no transit required.

›
Route Steps
1
Start at Brookland-CUA Metro
Head south on MBT toward NoMa.
2
Through NoMa toward Union Station (~2 mi)
Follow trail south, transition to streets near H St NE.
3
Run west along the National Mall (~4.5 mi)
Pass the Capitol, Washington Monument, Lincoln Memorial. Flat, paved.
Best run in DC — especially at sunrise
4
Finish & catch train home
Union Station, Smithsonian, or L'Enfant Plaza — multiple options nearby.
